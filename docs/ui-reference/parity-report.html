Soul Player: old React/Tauri app vs the Rune -ng head

Both apps driven live over the same library (testing-audio-library, 13 albums / 9 artists / 68 tracks). The old desktop app captured over CDP; -ng rendered headlessly via --snap. Page-by-page, at matched widths.

The two apps share the same bones — sidebar with dot-nav, a “NOW PLAYING” eyebrow, the bottom transport, the lavender accent. Structurally they already read as the same product.

The gap is richness. The old app is the more finished subset: per-page search + genre filters, full codec-badged track tables, blurred-art artist avatars, hover play-overlays and right-click menus, and reliably-resolved cover art. -ng is leaner and denser, and loses several covers to an art-extraction gap. The backlog below is what closing to 1:1 takes.

Page by page

Albums

  • REFSearch bar ("Search 14 albums…") + a Filters button that reveals genre chips.
  • REF5-column grid, larger cards; every cover loaded.
  • -NGNo search / no Filters bar — jumps straight to the grid.
  • -NGDenser 7-column grid; card subtitle is “Artist · Year” (old shows artist only).
  • GAPSeveral -ng covers fail to load (placeholder note) that the old app resolved — an art-extraction gap.

Tracks

Interactions & states (old app)

Hover: album card

  • REFHovering an album cover reveals a play ▶ overlay and shifts the title opacity. Clicking the cover plays; clicking the title opens the detail page.

Right-click: context menu + Filters

  • REFRight-clicking an album opens a context menu (Add to Playlist, Edit Artwork). The Filters button expands genre chips (Ambient, Classical, Electronic, Folk, Hip-Hop, Jazz, Video Game).
