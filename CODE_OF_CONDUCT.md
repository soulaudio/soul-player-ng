# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a music player, not a place worth being unpleasant
over.

## What we expect

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are about facts, not character.
- Criticise code, not people. "This leaks a file handle" is useful; "this is
  garbage" is not.
- Accept that maintainers may say no, and say it without a long justification.
- Respect that contributors work on their own time.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind; sexualised language or
imagery; publishing anyone's private information; sustained disruption of
discussions; and any behaviour a reasonable person would call unwelcome.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere
else someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to sebastian.stupak@pm.me. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block
accounts. We will explain the reason where doing so does not expose the person who
reported it.
